Privacy Policy

Learn how our Discord Verification Bot collects, protects, and manages your data responsibly.

Effective Date: August 8, 2026
1

Introduction

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information our Discord Verification Bot ("Bot", "Service") collects when you authorize or use our verification tools, how that data is utilized, and your rights regarding data removal.

2

Information We Collect

To provide identity verification and role assignment services, our Bot collects only the minimum necessary public Discord information:

  • Discord User Identifier (ID): Used as a unique key to verify your identity across servers.
  • Discord Username & Display Name: Used to display your identity in verification records.
  • Avatar Hash / URL: Stored strictly for UI profile rendering.
  • Verification Timestamps & Status: Used to track when verification was completed.

We DO NOT collect: Passwords, payment information, private messages, or any sensitive personal credentials.

3

How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Verifying user identity to grant appropriate server access and roles.
  • Preventing duplicate accounts, spamming, and raid attempts in protected servers.
  • Maintaining security logs for system troubleshooting and audit purposes.
4

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in secure, encrypted databases protected by industry-standard security protocols. Access to database records is restricted exclusively to authorized bot administrators.

5

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We operate under a strict NO-SELL POLICY. We will never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third-party advertisers or external commercial services under any circumstances.

6

User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your stored verification data. You may request complete deletion of your records from our systems at any time:

  • Unlinking your Discord account through the web dashboard.
  • Contacting our support team directly to request manual database wipe.
7

Discord Policy Compliance

Our Service fully complies with the Discord Developer Terms of Service and Discord Developer Policy. We adhere strictly to API usage guidelines and data privacy principles set by Discord Inc.
